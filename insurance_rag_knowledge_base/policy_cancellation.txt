SECURELIFE INSURANCE - POLICY CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

FREE LOOK PERIOD
Eligible new policyholders may cancel a policy during a free look period of generally 15 days from receipt of the policy document, subject to product rules.

FREE LOOK REFUND
A refund may be calculated after deducting charges for the period of coverage, administrative costs, medical examination expenses where applicable, and other permitted deductions.

CANCELLATION AFTER FREE LOOK
Cancellation after the free look period may be allowed according to policy terms. The refund amount, if any, can depend on policy type, elapsed coverage period, claims history, outstanding obligations, and applicable cancellation charges.

HOW TO REQUEST CANCELLATION
Customers may be asked to provide:
- Written cancellation request
- Policy number
- Identity proof
- Reason for cancellation where required
- Bank details for eligible refunds

EFFECTIVE DATE
Coverage generally ends on the cancellation effective date specified by SecureLife.

CLAIMS AND REFUNDS
Existing claims or losses may affect cancellation and refund calculations.

IMPORTANT
Customers should consider replacement coverage before cancelling an active policy to avoid an unintended protection gap.
