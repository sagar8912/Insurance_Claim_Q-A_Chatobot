SECURELIFE PERSONAL ACCIDENT INSURANCE

OVERVIEW
Personal accident insurance provides financial benefits for specified accidental events resulting in death, permanent disability, or other covered outcomes.

ACCIDENTAL DEATH
A lump-sum benefit may be payable if an insured person dies as a direct result of a covered accident, subject to policy conditions.

PERMANENT TOTAL DISABILITY
Benefits may be available when a covered accident directly causes permanent total disability as defined by the policy.

PERMANENT PARTIAL DISABILITY
The benefit amount may depend on the severity and type of disability according to the benefit schedule.

TEMPORARY TOTAL DISABILITY
Some plans may provide periodic benefits during a covered period of temporary inability to work, subject to waiting periods and medical certification.

MEDICAL EVIDENCE
Claims may require medical reports, diagnostic records, accident reports, identity documents, and other evidence.

EXCLUSIONS
Benefits may not apply to intentional injury, certain illegal activities, intoxication-related incidents, excluded sports, war-related events, or non-accidental illnesses unless specifically covered.

REPORTING
The insured or representative should notify SecureLife promptly and cooperate with medical and claim assessment.
