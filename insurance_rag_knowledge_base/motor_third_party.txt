SECURELIFE MOTOR INSURANCE - THIRD-PARTY LIABILITY

PURPOSE
Third-party motor insurance is designed to address legal liability arising from bodily injury, death, or property damage suffered by another person because of the insured vehicle, subject to applicable law and policy conditions.

WHAT THIRD-PARTY COVER DOES NOT NORMALLY INCLUDE
Third-party insurance generally does not pay for damage to the policyholder's own vehicle. Own damage protection usually requires comprehensive coverage or an applicable separate own-damage policy.

ACCIDENT REPORTING
The policyholder should report incidents promptly and provide accurate details. Legal notices, police reports, summons, or correspondence received from third parties should be forwarded according to policy requirements.

COOPERATION
The insured must cooperate with claim investigation and legal defense where applicable. The insured should not admit liability or agree to a settlement without authorization when the policy requires insurer involvement.

LIMITS
Liability payments may be subject to statutory requirements, policy limits, and legal determination.

DRIVER REQUIREMENTS
The vehicle should be driven by an authorized person holding a valid license appropriate for the vehicle type.

GEOGRAPHICAL USE
Coverage normally applies only within the geographical area stated in the policy schedule.

CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY
The policyholder should maintain valid registration and comply with legal requirements for vehicle operation.
