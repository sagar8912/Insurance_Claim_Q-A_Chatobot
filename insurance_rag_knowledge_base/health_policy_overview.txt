SECURELIFE HEALTH INSURANCE - POLICY OVERVIEW

PURPOSE OF HEALTH INSURANCE
Health insurance helps eligible insured persons manage certain medical expenses arising from illness, injury, hospitalization, and covered medical treatment. The exact coverage depends on the selected plan and policy schedule.

COMMON COVERED EXPENSES
Depending on the plan, eligible expenses may include hospitalization charges, room charges within applicable limits, doctor fees, surgeon fees, nursing charges, diagnostic tests, medicines used during covered hospitalization, operation theatre charges, intensive care expenses, ambulance services, and selected day-care procedures.

HOSPITALIZATION
Many plans require hospitalization for more than 24 consecutive hours unless the treatment is classified as a covered day-care procedure. Emergency hospitalization should be reported to SecureLife or its assistance partner as soon as reasonably possible.

PRE-HOSPITALIZATION EXPENSES
Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before a covered hospitalization may be considered when they are directly related to the illness or injury that resulted in hospitalization.

POST-HOSPITALIZATION EXPENSES
Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days after discharge may be considered when they are directly related to the covered hospitalization and supported by valid medical documentation.

SUM INSURED
The sum insured is the maximum amount available under the policy, subject to applicable sub-limits, deductibles, co-payments, and policy conditions. Payment of a claim may reduce the available sum insured depending on policy design.

FAMILY COVER
Some plans can cover multiple family members under a family floater arrangement. Eligibility and the persons covered are shown in the policy schedule.

CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES
The insured should provide accurate medical history when requested, retain medical records and bills, notify the insurer according to policy requirements, and cooperate with claim verification.

IMPORTANT
Not every medical expense is automatically covered. Coverage is subject to waiting periods, exclusions, sub-limits, medical necessity, policy terms, and available sum insured.
