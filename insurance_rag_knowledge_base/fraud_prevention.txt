SECURELIFE INSURANCE - FRAUD PREVENTION

WHAT IS INSURANCE FRAUD?
Insurance fraud involves intentionally providing false information or creating a deceptive situation to obtain an insurance benefit.

EXAMPLES
Examples include submitting altered bills, staging accidents, claiming for losses that did not occur, exaggerating damage, hiding material facts during application, or impersonating another person.

CUSTOMER PROTECTION
Customers should:
- Never share OTPs or account passwords.
- Verify official communication channels.
- Avoid paying money to unverified individuals claiming to be agents.
- Keep copies of policy documents and receipts.
- Report suspicious requests.

CLAIM INVESTIGATION
SecureLife may verify documents, contact service providers, inspect property, review records, and request additional evidence when fraud indicators are present.

FALSE DOCUMENTS
Submitting forged or altered documents can lead to claim rejection.

REPORTING SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY
Customers should report suspected scams or unauthorized use of their policy information through official customer support channels.

PRIVACY
Customers should provide sensitive documents only through approved channels.
