SECURELIFE LIFE INSURANCE - POLICY OVERVIEW

PURPOSE
Life insurance provides financial protection to nominated beneficiaries if the insured person dies during the covered period, subject to policy terms.

TERM LIFE INSURANCE
Term insurance provides protection for a defined policy term. If the insured dies during the active policy term and the claim is eligible, the death benefit is payable according to the policy.

WHOLE LIFE COVER
Whole life products may provide longer-term coverage, subject to the policy design, premium obligations, and conditions.

SUM ASSURED
The sum assured is the benefit amount defined in the policy schedule. The actual amount payable can depend on policy type, riders, outstanding obligations, and policy conditions.

PREMIUM PAYMENT
Premiums may be paid monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annually depending on the selected plan.

GRACE PERIOD
A grace period of 30 days may be available for eligible premium payments. Policy benefits during the grace period depend on product terms.

POLICY LAPSE
Failure to pay the required premium beyond the applicable grace period may cause the policy to lapse.

NOMINATION
The policyholder should nominate one or more beneficiaries according to applicable policy rules. Nomination information should be kept current.

CLAIM
The nominee or legal claimant may submit a death claim with required documentation including death certificate, policy information, identity documents, bank details, and other records requested during verification.
