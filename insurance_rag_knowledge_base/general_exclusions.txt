SECURELIFE INSURANCE - GENERAL EXCLUSIONS

INTENTIONAL LOSS
Loss or damage intentionally caused by the insured may not be covered.

FRAUD
Claims based on false information, forged documents, staged incidents, or deliberate misrepresentation may be denied and may lead to further action.

ILLEGAL ACTIVITY
Losses arising directly from illegal activity may be excluded.

WAR AND CIVIL DISTURBANCE
War, invasion, rebellion, civil unrest, or similar events may be excluded unless specifically covered.

WEAR AND TEAR
Normal deterioration, gradual damage, and routine maintenance expenses are generally not insurance events.

FAILURE TO FOLLOW CONDITIONS
Failure to meet important reporting, disclosure, maintenance, or safety requirements may affect coverage.

ALCOHOL OR PROHIBITED SUBSTANCES
Claims arising in prohibited circumstances involving alcohol or drugs may be excluded depending on the product and facts.

POLICY-SPECIFIC TERMS
General exclusions do not replace product-specific exclusions. The policy schedule and wording always determine applicable coverage.
