SECURELIFE SMALL BUSINESS INSURANCE - OVERVIEW

PURPOSE
Small business insurance helps eligible businesses manage financial losses from selected risks affecting property, operations, liability, and employees.

PROPERTY COVER
Coverage may protect business premises, equipment, furniture, inventory, and other declared assets against specified events such as fire or selected natural disasters.

BUSINESS INTERRUPTION
Where included, business interruption coverage may help with specified financial losses caused by a covered property event that disrupts operations.

PUBLIC LIABILITY
Public liability coverage may respond to legal liability for accidental injury or property damage suffered by third parties, subject to policy limits.

EMPLOYEE ACCIDENT COVER
Optional employee accident benefits may be available for covered occupational accidents.

CYBER RISK
Certain business products may offer limited cyber protection for specified events such as data incidents or business interruption caused by a covered cyber event.

RISK INFORMATION
Businesses should accurately disclose industry, turnover, locations, assets, security controls, prior losses, and other underwriting information.

EXCLUSIONS
Professional services liability, intentional acts, known losses, contractual penalties, and other specialized risks may require separate coverage.
