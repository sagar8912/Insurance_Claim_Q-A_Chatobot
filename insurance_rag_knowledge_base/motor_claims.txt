SECURELIFE MOTOR INSURANCE - CLAIMS PROCESS

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS AFTER AN ACCIDENT
1. Move to a safe location if possible.
2. Check whether anyone requires medical assistance.
3. Contact emergency services when necessary.
4. Record the date, time, location, and circumstances.
5. Take photographs or videos if safe.
6. Exchange legally required information with involved parties.
7. Notify SecureLife as soon as possible.

DOCUMENTS
Commonly requested documents include policy number, driving license, vehicle registration certificate, photographs, repair estimate, accident description, police report where applicable, and bank details for reimbursement.

INSPECTION
For own-damage claims, SecureLife may arrange inspection before repair. Customers should preserve damaged parts when requested.

GARAGE REPAIR
Cashless repair may be available at participating network garages. Final settlement remains subject to policy coverage and approved repair costs.

THEFT
For theft, immediate police reporting is important. Additional ownership and key documents may be requested.

CLAIM DECISIONS
A claim may be approved, partially approved, or rejected. Reasons may include coverage limits, deductibles, depreciation, exclusions, insufficient evidence, or policy conditions.

NO CLAIM BONUS
A No Claim Bonus may be available at renewal for eligible claim-free periods according to policy rules. Making certain claims can affect future bonus eligibility.
