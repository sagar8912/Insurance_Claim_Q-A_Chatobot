SECURELIFE MOTOR INSURANCE - COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE

OVERVIEW
Comprehensive motor insurance provides protection for selected loss or damage to the insured vehicle in addition to applicable third-party liability protection.

OWN DAMAGE
Subject to policy terms, own damage coverage may respond to accidental collision, fire, theft, malicious damage, certain natural events, and other covered incidents.

NATURAL EVENTS
Depending on policy wording, covered events may include flood, storm, cyclone, earthquake, lightning, or other specified natural perils.

THEFT
A theft claim generally requires prompt notification to police and SecureLife. The customer may be asked to submit a police report, vehicle registration information, keys, policy details, and other ownership documents.

ACCIDENT DAMAGE
After an accident, the driver should ensure safety, obtain emergency assistance if necessary, document the incident, and report the claim promptly. Repairs should not normally begin before required inspection or authorization.

DEDUCTIBLE
A deductible is the portion of an eligible loss that remains the responsibility of the policyholder. Deductible amounts are shown in the policy schedule.

SUM INSURED OR INSURED DECLARED VALUE
Settlement for total loss may depend on the insured declared value or other valuation method described in the policy.

ADD-ONS
Optional benefits may include roadside assistance, zero depreciation protection, engine protection, consumables cover, or other features. Add-ons are covered only when specifically selected.

IMPORTANT EXCLUSIONS
Coverage may not apply to normal wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, driving without a valid license, intentional damage, unauthorized use, or driving under prohibited circumstances.
