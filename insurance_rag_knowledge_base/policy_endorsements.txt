SECURELIFE INSURANCE - POLICY ENDORSEMENTS AND CHANGES

WHAT IS AN ENDORSEMENT?
An endorsement is an official change or addition to an existing insurance policy. The endorsement becomes part of the policy documentation.

COMMON CHANGES
Customers may request changes to:
- Address
- Contact number
- Email address
- Nominee
- Vehicle information
- Insured family members
- Selected add-ons
- Sum insured where permitted

DOCUMENTS
Supporting documents may be required depending on the requested change.

PREMIUM IMPACT
Some endorsements may increase or decrease the premium. SecureLife will communicate any additional payment or eligible refund.

EFFECTIVE DATE
A change may take effect immediately, from a specified future date, or after approval depending on underwriting requirements.

MATERIAL CHANGES
Customers should disclose material changes that could affect risk. Examples may include changes in vehicle usage, property occupancy, or other insured circumstances.

CONFIRMATION
Customers should review the revised policy schedule after an endorsement to confirm that the requested change has been correctly recorded.

RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE NOTES
