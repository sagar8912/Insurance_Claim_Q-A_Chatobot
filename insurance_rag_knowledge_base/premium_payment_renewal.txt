SECURELIFE INSURANCE - PREMIUM PAYMENT AND RENEWAL

PREMIUM
The premium is the amount paid to maintain insurance coverage. The amount and payment schedule are shown in the policy schedule.

PAYMENT FREQUENCY
Available frequencies may include monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and annual payments depending on product eligibility.

PAYMENT METHODS
Customers may pay through debit card, credit card, UPI, net banking, bank transfer, or other approved digital methods.

DUE DATE
Premium should be paid on or before the due date to avoid interruption of coverage.

GRACE PERIOD
Certain products provide an additional grace period. For eligible life insurance products, a 30-day grace period may apply. The exact rule depends on the policy.

RENEWAL
Customers should renew policies before expiry to maintain continuity of coverage. Renewal terms, premiums, and underwriting requirements may change according to product rules.

LATE RENEWAL
A policy renewed after expiry may require additional declaration, inspection, medical information, or underwriting review.

LAPSED POLICY
A lapsed policy may not provide active coverage. Revival or reinstatement may require payment of outstanding premiums and satisfaction of additional requirements.

PAYMENT RECEIPTS
Customers should retain payment confirmation and review the updated policy schedule after renewal.
