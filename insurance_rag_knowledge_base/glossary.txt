SECURELIFE INSURANCE GLOSSARY

BENEFICIARY: Person entitled to receive a policy benefit according to policy terms.
CLAIM: Formal request for payment or service under an insurance policy.
CLAIMANT: Person submitting a claim.
CO-PAYMENT: Portion of an eligible expense paid by the insured.
DEDUCTIBLE: Amount of loss paid by the policyholder before or alongside insurer payment.
ENDORSEMENT: Official modification to an insurance policy.
EXCLUSION: Event, expense, or circumstance not covered by the policy.
GRACE PERIOD: Additional time allowed for premium payment under applicable policy terms.
INSURED: Person, property, or interest protected by insurance.
INSURER: Company providing insurance protection.
NETWORK HOSPITAL: Hospital participating in a cashless treatment arrangement.
NOMINEE: Person designated under applicable rules to receive a life insurance benefit.
POLICY: Contract describing insurance coverage.
POLICYHOLDER: Person who owns the insurance policy.
PREMIUM: Amount paid for insurance coverage.
PRE-AUTHORIZATION: Preliminary review for cashless medical treatment.
PRE-EXISTING DISEASE: Medical condition existing before policy commencement according to policy definitions.
RIDER: Additional benefit attached to a base policy.
SUM INSURED: Maximum amount of protection available under many general insurance policies.
SUM ASSURED: Defined benefit amount under many life insurance policies.
WAITING PERIOD: Period during which specified coverage is not yet available.
