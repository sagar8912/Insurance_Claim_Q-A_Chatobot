SECURELIFE INSURANCE - CLAIM REJECTION AND APPEAL INFORMATION

REASONS A CLAIM MAY BE DECLINED
A claim may be rejected when:
1. The incident is outside policy coverage.
2. The policy was inactive or expired.
3. A waiting period had not been completed.
4. The loss falls under an exclusion.
5. Required documents were not provided.
6. The claim amount exceeds applicable limits.
7. Material information was false or misleading.
8. Policy conditions were not followed.
9. Fraud is suspected or established after investigation.

PARTIAL APPROVAL
A claim can be partially approved when only part of the submitted expense or loss is eligible. Examples include deductibles, sub-limits, depreciation, non-covered items, or amounts above the policy limit.

REQUESTING EXPLANATION
Customers may request clarification of a claim decision and the relevant policy provisions.

REVIEW OR APPEAL
Where the claims process allows, customers may submit additional documents or request a review. A review request should clearly identify the claim reference number, disputed decision, and supporting information.

COMPLAINT ESCALATION
If the customer remains dissatisfied, the customer may use SecureLife's formal complaint process and external dispute mechanisms where available under applicable regulations.

RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE NOTES
