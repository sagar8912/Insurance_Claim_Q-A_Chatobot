SECURELIFE HOME INSURANCE - COVERAGE OVERVIEW

PURPOSE
Home insurance protects eligible residential buildings and household contents against specified risks.

BUILDING COVER
Building coverage may include structural damage caused by covered events such as fire, specified natural disasters, explosion, or other insured perils.

CONTENTS COVER
Household contents may include furniture, appliances, electronics, and personal belongings, subject to ownership evidence and coverage limits.

THEFT
Theft or burglary may be covered when included in the selected policy. A police report and evidence of ownership may be required.

WATER DAMAGE
Certain sudden and accidental water damage may be covered. Damage resulting from long-term neglect, poor maintenance, or gradual leakage may be excluded.

NATURAL DISASTERS
Depending on the selected policy, flood, earthquake, storm, cyclone, and lightning damage may be covered.

CLAIM DOCUMENTATION
Customers should take photographs, prevent further damage where safe, preserve evidence, report theft to police, and submit repair estimates or invoices when requested.

EXCLUSIONS
Common exclusions may include normal wear and tear, poor maintenance, intentional damage, gradual deterioration, illegal activities, and events specifically excluded by policy wording.
