SECURELIFE LIFE INSURANCE - NOMINEE AND DEATH CLAIMS

NOMINEE
A nominee is a person designated by the policyholder to receive policy benefits according to applicable policy rules following the death of the insured.

UPDATING NOMINATION
Policyholders may request changes to nominee information subject to product rules and required documentation. Life events such as marriage, divorce, or changes in family circumstances may make an update appropriate.

DEATH CLAIM NOTIFICATION
The claimant should notify SecureLife as soon as reasonably possible after the death of the insured.

COMMON DOCUMENTS
Documents may include:
- Claim form
- Death certificate
- Policy document or policy number
- Identity proof of claimant
- Bank account details
- Medical records where relevant
- Hospital records where relevant
- Additional legal documents when requested

VERIFICATION
SecureLife may verify policy status, premium history, cause and date of death, identity of the claimant, nomination details, and other relevant information.

CLAIM OUTCOME
After verification, the claim may be approved, additional information may be requested, or the claim may be declined if policy conditions are not satisfied.

MISREPRESENTATION
Material false statements or non-disclosure during policy application may affect claim eligibility according to applicable law and policy terms.
