SECURELIFE TRAVEL INSURANCE - POLICY OVERVIEW

PURPOSE
Travel insurance provides protection against selected unexpected events occurring during covered domestic or international travel.

MEDICAL EMERGENCIES
Eligible emergency medical treatment may be covered during travel, subject to policy limits and conditions. Customers should contact the emergency assistance service as soon as possible when hospitalization is required.

TRIP CANCELLATION
Trip cancellation benefits may apply when a covered event prevents the insured from beginning a planned journey. Covered reasons may include serious illness, accident, death of an immediate family member, or specified natural disasters.

TRIP INTERRUPTION
Benefits may be available when a covered event requires the trip to be shortened or interrupted.

BAGGAGE
Coverage may include loss, theft, or delay of checked baggage, subject to limits and documentation requirements.

PASSPORT LOSS
Assistance may be available for certain costs arising from loss of a passport. The traveler should report the loss to local authorities and contact the relevant embassy or consulate.

EMERGENCY EVACUATION
Where included, the policy may provide assistance for medically necessary emergency evacuation.

COMMON EXCLUSIONS
Travel against official government advice, illegal activity, negligence, certain pre-existing conditions, and unapproved adventure sports may not be covered.

DOCUMENTS
Travel itinerary, boarding passes, receipts, airline reports, medical reports, police reports, and proof of expenses may be required.
