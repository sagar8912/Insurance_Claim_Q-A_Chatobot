SECURELIFE HEALTH INSURANCE - CASHLESS HOSPITALIZATION AND REIMBURSEMENT

CASHLESS TREATMENT
Cashless treatment is available only at participating network hospitals and is subject to authorization. Cashless treatment means the insurer or claims administrator may directly settle eligible expenses with the hospital, while the customer remains responsible for non-covered expenses, deductibles, co-payments, and amounts exceeding policy limits.

PLANNED HOSPITALIZATION
For planned treatment, the customer should contact the hospital insurance desk before admission. The hospital may submit a pre-authorization request containing diagnosis, treatment details, estimated costs, and policy information.

EMERGENCY HOSPITALIZATION
For emergency admission, the hospital should be informed that the patient has SecureLife coverage as soon as practical. A pre-authorization request may then be submitted after admission.

PRE-AUTHORIZATION
Approval of pre-authorization is not always a final guarantee of complete claim payment. Final settlement depends on treatment details, actual expenses, policy coverage, exclusions, and verification.

DOCUMENTS
The hospital or customer may need to provide identity proof, health card or policy details, medical records, diagnostic reports, discharge summary, prescriptions, and other relevant information.

REIMBURSEMENT CLAIMS
If treatment occurs at a non-network hospital or cashless approval is unavailable, the customer may pay the hospital and later submit a reimbursement claim. Original bills, receipts, discharge summary, medical reports, prescriptions, and claim forms may be required.

NON-COVERED AMOUNTS
The customer may need to pay for excluded services, convenience items, excess room charges, deductibles, co-payments, and expenses above policy limits.
