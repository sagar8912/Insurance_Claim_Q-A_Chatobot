SECURELIFE HEALTH INSURANCE - COMMON EXCLUSIONS

GENERAL PRINCIPLE
Health insurance covers only eligible expenses described in the policy. The following are examples of common exclusions or restrictions.

COSMETIC OR AESTHETIC TREATMENT
Purely cosmetic procedures may not be covered unless medically necessary because of an accident, illness, or reconstructive requirement specifically covered by the policy.

SELF-INFLICTED INJURY
Treatment arising from intentional self-inflicted injury may be excluded subject to applicable policy wording and legal requirements.

SUBSTANCE ABUSE
Treatment primarily related to alcohol or prohibited substance abuse may be excluded unless a specific benefit provides coverage.

EXPERIMENTAL TREATMENT
Experimental, investigational, or unproven treatments may not be covered unless specifically approved under the policy.

NON-PRESCRIBED SERVICES
Services that are not medically necessary or not prescribed by an appropriately qualified medical practitioner may be excluded.

PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS
Claims related to pre-existing diseases may be restricted until the applicable waiting period has been completed.

ROUTINE OR PERSONAL EXPENSES
Administrative fees, personal comfort items, non-medical supplies, and similar expenses may not be reimbursable.

POLICY-SPECIFIC EXCLUSIONS
Customers should consult their policy wording because exclusions can vary by product and endorsement.
