SECURELIFE INSURANCE - GENERAL CLAIMS PROCESS

STEP 1: NOTIFY SECURELIFE
Report the incident as soon as possible using the customer portal, customer service, emergency assistance channel, or another approved reporting method.

STEP 2: REGISTER THE CLAIM
Provide policy number, insured name, incident date, location where relevant, description of the event, contact information, and preliminary loss details.

STEP 3: DOCUMENT SUBMISSION
Required documents vary by product. SecureLife may request claim forms, photographs, medical reports, police reports, invoices, receipts, ownership evidence, repair estimates, or other supporting records.

STEP 4: ASSESSMENT
SecureLife reviews policy validity, coverage, facts of the incident, documentation, exclusions, deductibles, limits, and other conditions.

STEP 5: ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
The claims team may contact the customer, hospital, garage, surveyor, service provider, or other relevant parties to obtain further information.

STEP 6: DECISION
The claim may be approved, partially approved, pending further information, or rejected.

PROCESSING TIME
Straightforward claims may be processed within approximately 7 to 15 business days after receipt of all required documents. Complex claims may take longer because of investigation or verification.

CLAIM STATUS
Customers may check status using their claim reference number, policy number, or registered contact details.

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
SecureLife may communicate requests and decisions through the portal, email, SMS, or other registered contact channels.

RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE NOTES
